FAQs

What are the pricing packages like?

Prices depend on the type of event, how many hours or days you need and whether you want photos, video or both. Send your details through the booking page and Dreas will reply on WhatsApp with a clear quote.

Where in Nigeria do you cover?

We are based in Awka, Anambra and cover events across the state and the South East. We also travel to other parts of Nigeria. Travel costs are agreed before the booking is confirmed.

How can I book you for multiple events?

On the booking page choose "Multiple days" and pick each date on the calendar. You can add details for each event in the notes and we will plan the coverage with you.

Can I book you through your social media handle?

Yes. You can send a message on Instagram or WhatsApp. The booking page is the quickest way though, because it shows the dates that are still open.
