    04How we work

    We film what happened, not a performance of it.

    Plan the day, then stay out of the way

    We agree the timeline and the must-have moments before the day, so on the day we can stay quiet and watch.

    Light first

    Halls are often dark and lit with mixed colours. We bring our own light and fast lenses, so skin looks like skin, not orange.

    Edit for the people in it

    The film is cut for you and your family to watch again in ten years, not for a trend. Social media cuts come separately.
