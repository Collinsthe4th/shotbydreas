Who We Are

SHOTBYDREAS is a photo and video team from Awka, Anambra, led by Dreas.

We started by filming friends’ birthdays and family events, and grew into a team that covers weddings, traditional marriages, funerals, thanksgivings and grand openings across the South East and beyond.

Our aim is simple. We want you to enjoy your day while we quietly catch the moments you will want to see again: the laughter, the dancing, the speeches and the small details in between.

We also run mobile photoshoots for portraits, pre-wedding content and brands, so you can get great pictures without booking a studio.
