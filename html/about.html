About

The person behind the camera, how booking works, and answers to common questions.

SHOTBYDREAS is the work of Dreas, a videographer and photographer based in Awka, Anambra State.

He films weddings, traditional marriages, birthdays, funerals and openings, and runs short mobile shoots for portraits and pre-wedding content. The aim on every job is the same: a film that feels like the day did.

Good to know

What are your prices?

Prices depend on the hours, the number of cameras and what you need delivered. Send your date and event on the booking page and Dreas will reply on WhatsApp with a quote.

Where in Nigeria do you work?

We are based in Awka and work across Anambra and the South-East. Events elsewhere in Nigeria are welcome, with travel and accommodation added to the quote.

Can I book you for more than one day?

Yes. On the booking page choose “Multiple days” and pick up to five dates, for example a traditional marriage and a white wedding on separate days.

Can I book through social media?

Yes, you can message on Instagram or WhatsApp. The booking page is the quickest way, because it shows which dates are already taken.
